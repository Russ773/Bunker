# Privacy policy

Bunker is a desktop app that runs on your own PC. It doesn't collect, store on any server, or share personal information, and it has no analytics or tracking.

**What stays on your PC**
- Your settings, saved server details and the player database Bunker builds from your own server. They are kept in your Windows user profile.
- RCON passwords are encrypted with Windows DPAPI, so only your Windows account can read them.

**Where Bunker connects**
- **Your own Rust server**, at the address and port you enter, over WebRCON.
- **GitHub** (github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com), to check for updates in the downloadable version and to browse the community widget library when you open it. GitHub's privacy policy applies to those requests.

Bunker sends nothing else to anyone. Source code: https://github.com/Russ773/Bunker

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Russ773/Bunker/issues
